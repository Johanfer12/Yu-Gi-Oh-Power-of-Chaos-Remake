## v0.1.0-alpha.22

- Joey: cards placed by an effect into a spell/trap or field zone now appear in that zone instead of the fifth monster zone.
- Joey: the field-spell zone uses Joey's own position, slightly different from Yugi and Kaiba.

Android: update from the launcher; the APK downloads inside the app and Android asks you to confirm the installation. Keep the app installed to retain your imported PCK and saves. Android26 uses the same package and signing certificate as Android25. No new physical-device validation is claimed.

Joey: las cartas colocadas por efecto en una zona de magia/trampa o de campo ya aparecen en esa zona y no en la quinta de monstruo, y la zona de campo usa la posición propia de Joey. En Android, actualiza desde el launcher; no desinstales la app ni borres sus datos.
